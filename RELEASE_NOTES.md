# PlayerTrack Enhanced 3.8.0.0

- Access is checked for M'aya Noh, Naomi Yoshizawa and Leon Glaves when logging in or changing character.
- A temporary notice shows whether verification passed or failed.
- New updates show a feature spotlight and the previous five releases until you dismiss the screen.
- Reopen release history from Settings → About → What's new.

## Previous five releases

### 3.7.8.0 — PTOM sound
- Hear the original PTOM sound when someone starts targeting you.
- Sound is on by default and can be switched off in PTOM or its pop-out.

### 3.7.7.0 — Compact PTOM window
- Pop out names and targeting times into a small independent window.
- Keep watching with the main window closed; the pop-out remembers its layout and open state.

### 3.7.6.0 — Smoother browsing
- Browse and search large saved player lists faster.
- Full-data exports do more work in the background to keep the interface responsive.

### 3.7.5.0 — Reliable player views
- Player details follow your most recent selection.
- Recent-player lists correctly expire old entries.

### 3.7.4.0 — Share alt links
- Export linked alts and import a friend's shared links.
- Preview conflicts and add missing linked characters without importing meetings or personal history.
- Undo your latest import without removing other links.
